## 2. About the Company

### Short description (about 40 words)

Classudo Technologies is a Lucknow-based digital marketing and IT agency serving businesses across India. It combines SEO, paid advertising, social media, web and app development, and graphic design to help brands grow their visibility, generate leads, and earn measurable results.

### Medium description (about 100 words)

Classudo Technologies is a digital marketing agency headquartered in Lucknow with an office in Noida (Delhi/NCR). The company brings together marketers, developers, and designers who deliver strategy and execution under one roof. Its services span search engine optimization, search engine marketing, pay-per-click advertising, social media and influencer marketing, YouTube growth, link building, lead generation, fundraising campaigns, website and software development, mobile app development, and creative design. Classudo focuses on performance: campaigns are built around a client's specific goals and measured on real outcomes such as traffic, leads, and sales, with an emphasis on sustainable, long-term growth.

### Company story and philosophy (from the About page)

- The name stands for "Classy": using the latest trends and technologies while keeping classic principles as the foundation.
- The agency describes itself as a leading digital marketing agency in India with a deep understanding of the Indian market.
- Its team is described as skilled and experienced, known for creativity and strategic execution, and mentored by industry experts.
- Campaigns are strategic and customized, aimed at connecting brands with target audiences, increasing online visibility, and generating leads.
- Guiding belief: the client should earn first, and the agency second.
- For graphic design, the stated approach is to deliver "creatives, not just images."
- For app development, the stated approach is to build apps that actually get downloaded and used, guided by a strategic blueprint, rather than apps that just sit in an app store.
- For web development, the stated approach is robust builds with customized features based on client requirements.

### Homepage positioning message (paraphrased)

In India's competitive online market, many businesses struggle with low search rankings, weak traffic, and ineffective marketing tactics. Classudo positions itself as the partner that fixes this by boosting visibility, engagement, and performance so brands succeed and generate leads. The homepage also points out that leading global brands (Amazon, Nike and Coca-Cola are named) grew through digital channels such as social media, content marketing, SEO, and paid advertising.

## 15. Verify Before Publishing

Items that are missing or inconsistent on the current site and worth fixing in the new one:

1. **"100% results-guaranteed #1 rank" claim.** The homepage meta description promises this, but the SEO page FAQ says no genuine agency can guarantee #1 rankings. Search engines and advertising standards bodies frown on ranking guarantees. Consider removing or rewording it.
2. **Conflicting numbers.** "200+ projects" (Digital Marketing), "200+ websites" (Web, App, and Graphic Design pages, where it likely should not appear at all), "150+ web apps" (meta), and "20+ developers" (meta). Confirm real figures and use one consistent set.
3. **Founding year and company history.** Not stated anywhere. Add year established, milestones, and registration details if you want them shown.
4. **Vision, mission, and core values.** None are formally written on the current site.
5. **Pricing and package details.** Domestic and International package pages exist but were not captured here. Pull the current prices from those pages.
6. **Client names and case studies.** Logos have no text names, and there are no numeric case studies (for example "traffic up 3x"). Adding these would strengthen credibility.
7. **Certifications and badges.** Confirm each is current and valid (Google Partner status, Semrush, Google Analytics, Bing Ads, Google Digital Garage, Make in India, Startup India). The badge images use inconsistent alt text (for example, the Semrush badge is labeled "google analytics certified partner").
8. **Typos on the live site.** "Clasudo Technology" on the About page, "Child Monetering App" in the Portfolio, and "Packing Design" in the Graphic Design service list (the URL says "packaging").
9. **Service page consistency.** "Performance Marketing" appears under the Web Development menu group, and White Label SEO is in the menu but missing from the Digital Marketing grid. Full Stack Development and UI Design appear on service pages but not in the mega menu.
10. **City landing pages.** Performance marketing pages exist for Delhi and Mumbai, but offices are in Lucknow and Noida. Consider clarifying service coverage.
11. **Two LinkedIn URLs.** The main site and blog footer point to different company pages. Pick one.
12. **Testimonials.** Use only testimonials you have permission to publish. Copy exact wording from the live page or from your own records, and consider adding photos, company names, and dates.
13. **Team page.** Confirm each person has agreed to be listed, and that titles and LinkedIn links are up to date. Two members have no LinkedIn link.
14. **Legal pages.** No Privacy Policy, Terms, or Refund Policy pages were found in the navigation. These are needed for forms, payments, and ad platforms.
15. **Company profile PDF.** It was too large to read here (over 30 MB). Review it yourself, since it may contain history, vision, or figures not on the website.

## 15. Verify Before Publishing

Items that are missing or inconsistent on the current site and worth fixing in the new one:

1. **"100% results-guaranteed #1 rank" claim.** The homepage meta description promises this, but the SEO page FAQ says no genuine agency can guarantee #1 rankings. Search engines and advertising standards bodies frown on ranking guarantees. Consider removing or rewording it.
2. **Conflicting numbers.** "200+ projects" (Digital Marketing), "200+ websites" (Web, App, and Graphic Design pages, where it likely should not appear at all), "150+ web apps" (meta), and "20+ developers" (meta). Confirm real figures and use one consistent set.
3. **Founding year and company history.** Not stated anywhere. Add year established, milestones, and registration details if you want them shown.
4. **Vision, mission, and core values.** None are formally written on the current site.
5. **Pricing and package details.** Domestic and International package pages exist but were not captured here. Pull the current prices from those pages.
6. **Client names and case studies.** Logos have no text names, and there are no numeric case studies (for example "traffic up 3x"). Adding these would strengthen credibility.
7. **Certifications and badges.** Confirm each is current and valid (Google Partner status, Semrush, Google Analytics, Bing Ads, Google Digital Garage, Make in India, Startup India). The badge images use inconsistent alt text (for example, the Semrush badge is labeled "google analytics certified partner").
8. **Typos on the live site.** "Clasudo Technology" on the About page, "Child Monetering App" in the Portfolio, and "Packing Design" in the Graphic Design service list (the URL says "packaging").
9. **Service page consistency.** "Performance Marketing" appears under the Web Development menu group, and White Label SEO is in the menu but missing from the Digital Marketing grid. Full Stack Development and UI Design appear on service pages but not in the mega menu.
10. **City landing pages.** Performance marketing pages exist for Delhi and Mumbai, but offices are in Lucknow and Noida. Consider clarifying service coverage.
11. **Two LinkedIn URLs.** The main site and blog footer point to different company pages. Pick one.
12. **Testimonials.** Use only testimonials you have permission to publish. Copy exact wording from the live page or from your own records, and consider adding photos, company names, and dates.
13. **Team page.** Confirm each person has agreed to be listed, and that titles and LinkedIn links are up to date. Two members have no LinkedIn link.
14. **Legal pages.** No Privacy Policy, Terms, or Refund Policy pages were found in the navigation. These are needed for forms, payments, and ad platforms.

## 15. Verify Before Publishing

Items that are missing or inconsistent on the current site and worth fixing in the new one:

1. **"100% results-guaranteed #1 rank" claim.** The homepage meta description promises this, but the SEO page FAQ says no genuine agency can guarantee #1 rankings. Search engines and advertising standards bodies frown on ranking guarantees. Consider removing or rewording it.
2. **Conflicting numbers.** "200+ projects" (Digital Marketing), "200+ websites" (Web, App, and Graphic Design pages, where it likely should not appear at all), "150+ web apps" (meta), and "20+ developers" (meta). Confirm real figures and use one consistent set.
3. **Founding year and company history.** Not stated anywhere. Add year established, milestones, and registration details if you want them shown.
4. **Vision, mission, and core values.** None are formally written on the current site.
5. **Pricing and package details.** Domestic and International package pages exist but were not captured here. Pull the current prices from those pages.
6. **Client names and case studies.** Logos have no text names, and there are no numeric case studies (for example "traffic up 3x"). Adding these would strengthen credibility.
7. **Certifications and badges.** Confirm each is current and valid (Google Partner status, Semrush, Google Analytics, Bing Ads, Google Digital Garage, Make in India, Startup India). The badge images use inconsistent alt text (for example, the Semrush badge is labeled "google analytics certified partner").
8. **Typos on the live site.** "Clasudo Technology" on the About page, "Child Monetering App" in the Portfolio, and "Packing Design" in the Graphic Design service list (the URL says "packaging").
9. **Service page consistency.** "Performance Marketing" appears under the Web Development menu group, and White Label SEO is in the menu but missing from the Digital Marketing grid. Full Stack Development and UI Design appear on service pages but not in the mega menu.
10. **City landing pages.** Performance marketing pages exist for Delhi and Mumbai, but offices are in Lucknow and Noida. Consider clarifying service coverage.
11. **Two LinkedIn URLs.** The main site and blog footer point to different company pages. Pick one.
12. **Testimonials.** Use only testimonials you have permission to publish. Copy exact wording from the live page or from your own records, and consider adding photos, company names, and dates.
13. **Team page.** Confirm each person has agreed to be listed, and that titles and LinkedIn links are up to date. Two members have no LinkedIn link.
14. **Legal pages.** No Privacy Policy, Terms, or Refund Policy pages were found in the navigation. These are needed for forms, payments, and ad platforms.
15. **Company profile PDF.** It was too large to read here (over 30 MB). Review it yourself, since it may contain history, vision, or figures not on the website.
